Privacy

soneeshk.com is Soneesh Kothagundla's personal website.

Messages. If you send a message, your email address and message are stored so Soneesh can read and reply. They are not shared or sold.

Sleep data. The site reads only Soneesh's own sleep total from his Google/Fitbit account, read-only, to show one number on the home page. No other health data is read, stored, or shared, and no visitor's Google data is ever accessed.

Questions or deletion requests: reply through the message box on the home page.
